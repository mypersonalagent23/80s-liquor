# Home

Scope: the public site (Home, Shop, Visit, About) for an unlaunched neighborhood package store. Mode: Persuade. The visitor decides to stop in or call.

Audience: someone in downtown Athens, often on a phone, checking if 80's Liquor is open. Job: read today's hours, call, or get directions. Proof: the verified address, hours, phone, Google rating with its date, and the tire-shop record. Constraints: white page, official logo used as-is, logo colors as accents only, three retro touches (stripe, skewed display line, work-order tickets), no invented facts, placeholders stay labeled, noindex.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A package store's front door, in the old tire shop on E Broad. The page refuses the wine-shop template of a centered tasting-note hero and a row of equal promo cards.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, ink #212123. Accents sampled from the official sign: pink #FB305D, yellow #FCBA00, teal #1ED2CC, cream #FCF6E4, one accent per region. Archivo Black expanded, skewed -8° for the one display line. Hanken Grotesk for body. IBM Plex Mono for hours and ticket labels. Hard 1px rules, one clipped corner on tickets, a three-line stripe as the only ornament.

STORY: The visitor sees a downtown package store in the old tire shop, gets today's hours and the phone in the header, and can open deals or directions before leaving the first screen. Unknown shelf, photos, and the owner stay labeled blank.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed three-color stripe, then logo left, nav, today's hours and phone right, one address line under a rule. Below, an asymmetric hero: left, the skewed headline "Downtown Athens' package store, in the old tire shop on E Broad.", the address, the weekly hours, a pink "This week's deals" button and an ink "Get directions" button. Right, a tall hatched frame labeled for the exterior photo. The next band starts on a desktop screen: deal tickets beside a larger mono hours block, with the building sentence as a thin full-width line, not a third card.

FORM: Brief-pinned shop-sign and work-order world. Seed key 2fffe3b2. The catalog hand (nixie, cracktro, night city, quilt, Massin, angura) loses to the pinned white page and the no-neon rule. Raises kept as discipline, not clothes: Massin's scale-as-voice (hours and phone larger than the story), the quilt's labeled provenance on every unknown, the counter's one live figure (open or closed). Angura's ray burst is refused (loaded rising-sun motif, and the brief bans neon clash).

SIGNATURE: The open/closed chip and today's row, from America/New_York. One entrance: the hero copy rises 8px and fades, only when motion is allowed. Text links redraw their underline from the left.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
