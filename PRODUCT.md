# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), static pages, deployed on Vercel with zero config. Specified in the build brief.

## Users

People in downtown Athens, GA deciding whether to walk or drive to 80's Liquor, and what time they can. They need today's hours, the phone number, and directions. Ash is publishing this as an unofficial portfolio concept; the store has not commissioned it.

## Product Purpose

A factual website for 80's Liquor: hours, phone, address, directions, and a plain account of the building. Success is a visitor who can call or get directions without being told anything the store has not confirmed. The site stays out of search indexes until an owner-approved launch.

## Positioning

A neighborhood package store's front door on the web. It shows the verified record and labels every unknown. It is not a shoppable catalog and not an official store site.

## Operating Context

Often a phone, near East Broad or the Firefly Trail trailhead, checking if the store is open before walking over. The useful artifacts are the hours, the phone number, and a directions link. No cart, account, form, or age gate.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: Home (`/`), Shop (`/shop`), Visit (`/visit`), About (`/about`).
- Shared header with logo, navigation, today's hours, and phone. Shared footer with address, phone, hours summary, and Facebook.
- No age gate, no forms, no e-commerce, no analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts, no embedded map iframe.
- Facts on the site come only from the verified list below. Anything else is a visible placeholder or is omitted.
- `noindex` until launch: meta robots, `X-Robots-Tag`, and `robots.txt` disallow.
- JSON-LD `LiquorStore` is not included in v1.
- Open and closed is computed in `America/New_York` from the verified hours.
- Footer carries "Internal test build v1" and states this is an unofficial portfolio concept.
- The official logo is used as supplied. It is not redrawn.

Open until the owner confirms (not stated as facts): permission to publish, owner name and story, opening or founding date, categories actually carried, weekly deals, photos, parking, payment methods, delivery, social accounts other than the known Facebook, holiday hours, whether an age notice is required, logo permission, and a custom domain.

## Brand Commitments

Binding constraints from the brief, not a visual system:

- Down-to-earth neighborhood liquor store. Not an upscale wine shop. Flatiron Wines is not a reference.
- White page. Colors from the official logo are accents only. A few 80s touches, not a neon theme.
- Header keeps hours and phone visible. Shop uses bold category labels and a corner tag on deals, in the spirit of Bottlerocket and Bottle King.
- The official logo file is used as-is.
- Plain local language. No tasting-note copy.

## Product Principles

- Hours and the phone number outrank story and decoration.
- If it is not in the verified list, it is a labeled placeholder or it is left out.
- The site is a portfolio concept until the owner says otherwise, and it must not look official.
- Someone outside the store, on a phone, should be able to call or get directions in one tap.
- Use the store's own facts and no hospitality filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on white. Visible focus, keyboard access, semantic pages, and a layout that works at 390px width. Yellow and the bright logo teal are fills and stripes, not small text. No age gate.
